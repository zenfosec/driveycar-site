How it plays

Three verbs. One held breath.

The car handles the gas, always. Everything you do fits in one hand, or on one wrist.

Steer

Drag or tilt to pick your line. Slide across lanes for the coins, or over the median when your side jams.

Overtake

Oncoming traffic is the fast way past. Cross, pass, get back before the gap closes.

Brake

You only get the one pedal. Hold for trains and crossing traffic, never for a red light.

Ways to drive

Highway is the main event. Three more come free.

Every mode keeps its own worldwide board. Scores from iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch land on the same one.

Highway · The main event

Eight lanes. Cross the median to overtake.

Your side jams, the other side has a gap. Take it. Highway never settles down: the lanes fill in, the pace lifts, and deep in a run the traffic starts changing lanes. Watch for the amber blinker.

Own worldwide board · iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch

Crossings

The gauntlet

Cross road after cross road. Hold to brake at the edge, let go into the gap, and never stop for a red light. Trains are another matter.

Two-Way

One lane each way
